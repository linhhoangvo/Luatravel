Before you go

Common questions

Planning a private holiday in Vietnam? Start here.

Can we change an itinerary?

Yes. Our tour ideas are starting points. We can discuss changes to the route, duration, activities and accommodation before preparing your quote.

Are your trips private?

We specialise in private tours and tailored arrangements. Your proposal will state the services and group arrangements included.

What is included in the quoted price?

It depends on the itinerary. We confirm accommodation, guides, transportation, meals, activities and exclusions clearly in each proposal.

Can travel agents work with Lua?

Yes. We can help travel professionals with itinerary design, local experiences and ground arrangements across Vietnam.
