About Lua Travel & Tours

Travel with a local perspective.

Thoughtfully planned journeys, genuine connections and a Vietnam we are proud to share.

Our story

Rooted in Vietnam.

The name Lúa means “rice” in Vietnamese — a familiar symbol of everyday life, nourishment and connection to the land. It is a simple reminder of where we come from.

From our home in Hoi An, we share Vietnam through its people, food, heritage and landscapes. Some journeys take you through busy cities and ancient towns; others leave time for quiet countryside, sea views and a slower pace.

Lua Travel & Tours brings these experiences together through private itineraries, carefully chosen stays, local tours and practical support on the ground.
