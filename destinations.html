Destinations / Vietnam

Vietnam, in all
its layers.

One country. Many ways to feel at home.

A note from Hoi An

There is no single way to see Vietnam.

A great journey might begin with coffee on a quiet street, lead you through mountains and old towns, and end with the sea. What matters is finding the places—and the pace—that feel right to you.

At Lúa, we bring together local knowledge and thoughtful planning, with space for discoveries along the way.

01 / THE NORTH

Mountain roads, river valleys and timeless cities

Begin among Hanoi’s lively streets, continue into quiet valleys and terraced hillsides, or spend a few days on the water in Ha Long Bay. The north rewards curiosity and a little time.

Hanoi↗Ha Long Bay↗Sapa↗Ha Giang↗Mai Chau↗Ninh Binh↗Pu Luong↗

02 / THE CENTRE

Where everyday life meets living heritage

Hoi An is home for our team. From here, discover the food, craft traditions and neighbourhoods we know best, then explore Hue’s history or venture towards Phong Nha’s limestone country.

03 / THE SOUTH

City energy, river rhythms and island days

Move between the fast pace of Ho Chi Minh City and the waterways of the Mekong Delta, or leave space to slow down by the sea. Southern Vietnam offers a different perspective with every stop.
