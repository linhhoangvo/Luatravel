Why visit Vietnam?

From the lively streets of Hanoi to Hoi An's lantern-lit lanes, Vietnam brings together remarkable landscapes, distinctive flavours and a rich sense of place.

Travel north to misty mountain villages and limestone bays, explore the heritage and countryside of Central Vietnam, or follow the rivers and markets of the Mekong Delta.

We believe the best journeys leave room for what interests you most. At Lúa, our Hoi An-based team helps shape private itineraries around your pace, interests and preferred way of travelling.

Types of tours in Vietnam

One journey can include food, nature, culture and time to unwind. You might spend a morning cycling through rice fields, an afternoon exploring historic streets and the next few days cruising between limestone islands.

We arrange private journeys, locally led day tours, wellness stays and cruise experiences. The route, accommodation and activities can be tailored to the people travelling, rather than asking everyone to follow the same itinerary.

Where to stay: North, Central & South

Vietnam's regions offer very different experiences. The North is known for mountain scenery, lakes, Hanoi's street food and Ha Long Bay. Central Vietnam combines heritage towns, coastal resorts and rural villages. In the South, Ho Chi Minh City's urban energy contrasts with the waterways of the Mekong and island beaches.

Rather than rushing through every destination, we recommend allowing enough time to enjoy the places that matter most to you.

Frequently asked questions about Vietnam

What is the best time to visit Vietnam?

Vietnam can be visited year-round, but weather varies considerably by region. Northern, Central and Southern Vietnam have different seasonal patterns, so the best months depend on where you'd like to go and what you'd like to do.

Can I visit Vietnam with my family?

Yes. Vietnam offers many family-friendly experiences, from cultural activities and gentle countryside outings to beaches and boat trips. We can discuss ages, pace and transport when tailoring your itinerary.

Is Vietnam safe for travellers?

Vietnam is a popular travel destination. As anywhere, use ordinary precautions with traffic, belongings, food and drinking water, and check current travel advice before departure.

Is Vietnam an expensive destination?

Vietnam offers accommodation and experiences across a broad range of budgets. Costs depend on the season, destinations, hotel category and whether you choose private arrangements.
