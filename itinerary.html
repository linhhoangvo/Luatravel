    Vietnam Adventure — 14 Days

    A sample north-to-south holiday through Hanoi, Mai Chau, Ninh Binh, Hoi An and Ho Chi Minh City. Heritage sites, village landscapes, local food and time to explore come together in one varied itinerary.

    Travel highlights

    • Explore Hanoi's Old Quarter and local food scene
    • Visit mountain villages and rice fields in Mai Chau
    • See limestone landscapes and waterways in Ninh Binh
    • Spend time in Hoi An and its neighbouring countryside
    • Finish with street food and city life in Ho Chi Minh City
