    Frequently asked questions about Vietnam

    Can I customise this itinerary?
    Yes. We can adjust the route, hotels, activities, travel pace and duration to suit your group.
    How many days do I need in Vietnam?
    Ten to fourteen days is a useful starting point for several regions. Longer stays allow more time in each destination.
    Are flights and hotels included?
    Only items specified in your confirmed quotation are included. Your proposal will list hotels, transfers, guides and any flights clearly.
    Can you arrange private guides?
    Local guides and private transport can be arranged as part of a tailored itinerary, subject to availability.
