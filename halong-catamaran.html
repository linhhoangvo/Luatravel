BEFORE YOU GO

Useful details.

Departure & transfers

All programmes depart from Tuan Chau Marina in Ha Long. Transfers from Hanoi may be included or arranged separately, depending on your booking.

Meals & activities

Day programmes include lunch; the evening programme features dinner. Kayaking, swimming and other water activities depend on the programme, weather and safety conditions.

Rates & availability

Vessel arrangements, inclusions and schedules may change. Our team will confirm the latest details and price for your dates before booking.
