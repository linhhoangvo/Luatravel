THE EXPERIENCE

Sail, explore, unwind.

The Halong Catamaran offers thoughtfully planned day cruises from Tuan Chau Marina through Ha Long Bay and Lan Ha Bay. Admire limestone scenery, relax on open sundecks and enjoy a programme of dining and activities on the water. The operator's documents describe vessels with capacity for up to 99 guests; exact vessel arrangements depend on the selected cruise.

CURATED JOURNEYS

Three ways to experience the bay.

Premium Day Cruise

A full-day journey balancing scenic cruising, outdoor activities and time to relax.

  • Boarding around 11:10–11:45

  • Kayaking or bamboo boat at Bright & Dark Cave

BEFORE YOU SAIL

A few useful details.

Where to board: Tuan Chau Marina, Ha Long, Quang Ninh. Transfers from Hanoi may be included or arranged separately, depending on your booking.

Meals: The day and morning programmes list lunch, while the evening programme features dinner. Other beverages are typically charged separately.

On the water: Kayaking, swimming, slides and other activities depend on the selected programme, weather and safety conditions.

Booking: The operator's documents describe different vessels and policies. We will confirm exact arrangements, inclusions and pricing for your date before booking.
