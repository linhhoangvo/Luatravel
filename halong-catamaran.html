HA LONG BAY · LAN HA BAY

The Halong Catamaran

A refined day on the water, surrounded by spectacular limestone scenery. Discover kayaking, swimming, time to relax and thoughtful onboard hospitality.

THE EXPERIENCE

Discover the bay, your way.

The Halong Catamaran offers day cruises from Tuan Chau Marina through the limestone landscapes of Ha Long and Lan Ha Bay. Enjoy elegant onboard spaces, open sundecks, lunch and activities on the water. The operator's brochure describes vessels accommodating up to 99 guests, with a choice of cruise experiences.

BEFORE YOU SAIL

Good to know.

Meeting point: Tuan Chau Marina, Ha Long, Quang Ninh. Hanoi transfers may be included or arranged separately depending on the selected package.

Food & drinks: Day-cruise documents list onboard lunch; dinner is offered on the evening cruise. Additional drinks and extras may incur charges.

Activities: Kayaking, swimming and water activities depend on weather, safety conditions and the chosen programme.

Rates: Please enquire for confirmed availability, pricing and current inclusions. The operator's documents contain differing vessel specifications and policies across programmes, so final details will be confirmed with the booking.
