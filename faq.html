Frequently Asked Questions

Planning a private holiday in Vietnam? Here are answers to a few common questions about travelling with Lúa.

Can we change an itinerary?

Yes. Our tour ideas are starting points. We can discuss changes to the route, duration, activities and accommodation before preparing your quote.

Are your trips private?

We specialise in private tours and tailored arrangements. Your proposal will state the services and group arrangements included.

What is included in the quoted price?

It depends on the itinerary. We confirm accommodation, guides, transportation, meals, activities and exclusions clearly in each proposal.

Can travel agents work with Lúa?

Yes. We help travel professionals with itinerary design, local experiences and ground arrangements across Vietnam. Contact us with your travel brief to discuss a tailored proposal.

How do I request a tailor-made trip?

Send us your preferred destinations, travel dates, number of guests and interests using our enquiry form. Our team can then discuss an itinerary that suits your plans.

Can you arrange hotels, transfers and cruises?

We can help coordinate accommodation, ground transportation, guides and selected cruise experiences as part of your travel arrangements. Availability and inclusions will be confirmed in your proposal.

When should we book?

Booking in advance allows more choice, especially for popular stays and peak travel dates. Tell us your dates and we can advise on available options.

Still have a question?

Our Hoi An-based team would be happy to help.
